Pure Data Centres to spend €1bn on Amsterdam's largest data centre
15 December 2025
Pure Data Centres plans to fund one of the largest hyperscale data centre investments announced in Europe this year, spending €1bn (US$1.17bn) on a campus in Amsterdam.
Construction of the campus will begin in Amsterdam in January 2026, with the company saying there will be a phased delivery from 2028 onwards. It will be able to serve either AI or cloud workloads, with a private substation powering the data centre.
Phase one will see the development of a substation and 30MW data centre with a total potential campus capacity of 70MW.
