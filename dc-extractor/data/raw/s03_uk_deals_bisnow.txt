Colt Data Centre Services, a London-headquartered company, said this week that it received planning committee approval from Hillingdon Council to expand its Hayes Digital Park campus in West London with three new hyperscale data centres.
Construction is expected to start in mid-2026, with the first data centre scheduled to go live in early 2029.
About 20 miles north, Ark Data Centres is planning a £2B data centre near Watford and has started a public consultation on the project. The site, a former Mercure hotel, could host a data centre campus with up to 200 MW.
