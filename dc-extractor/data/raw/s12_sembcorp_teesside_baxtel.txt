Sembcorp Plans a 280MW Data Center in Teesside, UK
May 21, 2026
Sembcorp is planning a 280MW data center at Wilton International in Teesside, UK, in partnership with Digital Reef. The project would include three buildings on a 154,553 sqm site acquired for £3.5 million ($4.7m) in July 2025.
