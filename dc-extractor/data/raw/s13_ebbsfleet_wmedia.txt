Clearstone unveils plans for 300 MW AI DC campus in Ebbsfleet
July 8, 2026
Clearstone, a UK infrastructure developer specializing in grid-connected infrastructure, has unveiled plans to build a 300 MW AI data center campus on a site spanning 145 acres near Dartford, on the borders of London and Kent.
The Ebbsfleet AI Data Center campus will be one of the first data center projects to follow the UK's Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project (NSIP) planning regime.
Clearstone applied to the Government in May 2026 for a direction under section 35 of the Planning Act 2008 to enable the project to be consented.
