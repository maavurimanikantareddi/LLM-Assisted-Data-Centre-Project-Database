A company co-founded by former Energy Secretary Rick Perry plans to build one of the world's largest data center complexes, which would be powered by nuclear, natural gas, solar and batteries.
Fermi America announced on Thursday its plans for a "HyperGrid" campus in Amarillo, Texas. The complex would contain the nation's largest nuclear and natural gas projects, aiming to deliver 1 gigawatt of power by the end of next year.
