Equinix revealed as occupant of £3.9B UK datacenter campus
Investment will fund 250 MW, three-facility campus near London as AI and cloud demand surge
The planning application for the project was filed last year by a company called DC01 UK Ltd. The site will feature three facilities with more than 250 MW capacity across 2 million square feet — making it one of Europe's largest datacenter campuses.
