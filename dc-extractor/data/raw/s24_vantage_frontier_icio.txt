Vantage Data Centers unveils plans for Frontier, a US$25B mega-campus in Texas
Campus will deliver 1.4 gigawatts of capacity in the Lone Star State, establishing a global AI hub.
Vantage Data Centers, a leading global provider of hyperscale data centers, has announced its largest investment to date: more than US$25 billion to develop a 1.4GW data center mega-campus in Shackelford County, Texas.
Located on 1,200 acres, Frontier will be Vantage's largest campus worldwide.
