Crusoe and Lancium Announce 1.0 Gigawatt AI Data Center Campus in Childress, Texas
Announcing a 270-acre, 1GW AI data center with solar power and water-efficient cooling; construction begins Q3 2026
The grid-connected campus spans 270 acres owned by Lancium, which will also develop and manage the site's energy infrastructure. The data center will be purpose-built to support the advanced AI computational requirements of a leading hyperscale technology company.
